Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 21, 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to ZenScan: Fast PDF Scanner ("ZenScan", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and web utility process information.

2. Local Document Processing (100% Offline)

ZenScan is built with a privacy-first, offline architecture. All document scans, camera captures, image enhancements, image contrast processing, and PDF generations are performed 100% locally on your device using client-side technologies.

We do not collect, store, transmit, or upload your scanned images or generated PDF files to any external cloud servers or databases.

3. Camera Access

To function as a document scanner, ZenScan requires access to your device's camera. Camera permission is used strictly for capturing document pages directly within the app interface. We do not access your camera in the background, nor do we record video or collect facial recognition data.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

To keep ZenScan free, we display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks, including Google AdMob and Google AdSense.

These third-party providers may use cookies, device identifiers, or web beacons to collect non-personally identifiable information (such as your device type, general geographic location, or advertising ID) to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our app and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. Children's Privacy

ZenScan does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. Because our core utility operates locally without requiring account registration, no personal profile data is gathered.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.
